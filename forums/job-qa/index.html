Job Q&A

Something failed. You have a photo, a code, or a voltage. Bring that — not a novel. Founding members get first crack at asking Matt in public.

How to ask

Give the tech something to hold.

  • Year / make / model and the system that quit
  • Symptoms, codes, and what you already tested
  • A photo of the bay, the label, or the screen

Advice here is in good faith. It is not a substitute for hands-on diagnosis on electrical, gas, or structural work. If it is urgent, Prefer Text and book a visit.
